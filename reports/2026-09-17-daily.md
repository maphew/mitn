**PR 11556, the "known CI noise" above, root-caused.** It is not noise
that comes and goes. `duplicate.spec.ts` fails all 4 attempts in 28 of the 138
Playwright runs since 2026-09-14T16:19Z (6 on `main`), and in 0 of the 102
runs before. Cause: `getByText("Note map").first()` is an inexact match that
also finds 2 CSS-hidden "Note Map" rows under `_hidden`. Playwright resolves
`.first()` 1 time and retries on that element. PR 11492 made the standalone
leader tab answer API calls in-page, so `waitUntil: "networkidle"` stopped
being a load barrier and the locator now resolves before the right row
exists on slow runners. Reproduced locally with CDP CPU throttling: 0 of 6
pass at 6x and 20x with the old locator, 6 of 6 with `exact: true`. Nemotron
review: no findings. Greptile: 5/5. Bead `mitn-d22`, memory
`trilium-e2e-hidden-row-locator`, artifacts `drafts/e2e-flake-2026-09-17/`.
