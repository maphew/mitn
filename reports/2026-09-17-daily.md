  `drafts/fix-batch-2026-09-16/xreview.sh`. Memories
  `codex-sol-unavailable`, `opencode-cross-vendor-review`.
- One Workflow per bug (same script, different `args`) gave incremental
  delivery: each pipeline notifies on its own, so PRs opened while others
  still ran. 26 parallel pipelines used a full 5-hour session limit in
  about 20 minutes; the failed stages resumed from cache after the reset
  with `resumeFromRunId`.
- Subagents started with the Agent tool inherit the orchestrator's
  current directory. A builder told to own worktree A stopped (correctly)
  when its cwd was worktree B. Give absolute paths and say that the
  inherited cwd can differ.
- About a third of "open bugs" were already fixed on main. A read-only
  triage stage before any worktree work paid for itself.
